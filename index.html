About

I am pursuing an M.I.A. and M.B.A. at Columbia University, with a focus on Data Science and Artificial Intelligence. My background in art history and visual culture at Oxford and the University of British Columbia informs my interdisciplinary approach to AI research. My interests lie at the intersection of embodied AI, algorithmic accountability, and the institutions that shape technology.

At Columbia University’s Creative Machines Lab, I contribute to research in creative robotics and embodied AI. As a Data and AI Fellow at the United Nations, I support AI-enabled data workflows for institutional analysis. My work connects computational methods with questions of culture, governance, and human experience.

Biography

My path began with art history: a B.A. (Honours) at the University of British Columbia, followed by an M.St. in History of Art and Visual Culture at Oxford. I later worked in banking and management research before pursuing international affairs and business at Columbia.

At Shanghai Pudong Development Bank, I worked on policy frameworks and data-supported workforce planning. At CEIBS, I contributed to business cases and white-paper research on entrepreneurship and internationalization. That experience continues to inform my research on AI, institutions, and technology governance.
